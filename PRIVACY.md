# Reach Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 13, 2026

Reach is an Android home screen app. This policy explains how it handles your information.

## Summary

Reach does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no internet access, ads or analytics.

## Information on your device

Reach stores your layout and settings on your device, including your pinned apps, app order, folders, widgets, appearance choices and the apps you last opened from search.

To work as a home screen, Reach reads the list of apps installed on your device and their shortcuts. This stays on your device.

## Notification dots

Notification dots are optional and off by default. Reach only asks for notification access if you turn them on.

If you allow access, Reach checks on your device which apps have notifications so it can show a dot on their icons. It does not save or share notifications or their content.

You can turn notification dots off at any time in Reach's settings, and remove notification access in your device's settings.

## Backup files

You can save a backup of your Reach setup to a file. Reach only makes one when you tap Back up, and saves it to the folder you pick. Reach has no internet access, so it can't send the file anywhere.

The file holds your settings, app layout, folders, pinned apps, the apps you last opened from search, and which widgets you placed and where. Apps are listed by their package names. It holds nothing from inside your apps, no notifications and nothing your widgets show.

The file is yours to keep, move, share or delete. It is not encrypted, so anyone you give it to can read what it holds.

When you restore a backup, Reach reads only the file you pick.

## Android backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may back up your Reach settings, pinned apps and app layout to your Google account. Reach has no access to that backup, and Google's privacy policy applies to it. Widgets are not included.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Reach deletes its data from your device. Backup files you saved are not deleted with it: delete them like any other file. You can manage Android backups in your device or Google account settings.

## Children

Reach is not directed at children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date.
